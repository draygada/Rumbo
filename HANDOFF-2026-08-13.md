## 1. The demo corpus is mostly missing, and the tutor hides it

**EDUC 475 (`canvas_course_221697`): 36 documents, 29 are stubs under 400
characters. All 7 survivors are administrative.**

Healthy: syllabus (4,735 chars), Group Project brief (5,219), Individual

Not one lecture. Not one case study. A stub's entire body is its own title:

```
canvas_lecture_2463880 → "Week 6 The Brazil Education Market: Week 6"
canvas_lecture_2434878 → "Week 1 Introduction to Entrepreneurship in Education: Canvas EDUC475 W26 Week 1 .pdf"
canvas_lecture_2443090 → "Week 4: Building Sustainable Education Ventures … GSV Ventures copy.pdf"
```

### Why it happened

`llamaparse_jobs`: 123 done, **27 errored**, all with the same failure.
Submitted 2026-07-20 04:36, marked failed 2026-08-11 07:40 — all 27 within
ninety seconds. The poller has a 30-minute staleness rule, so a three-week gap
means it simply was not running for 22 days. When it finally woke, every result
returned `result fetch 404`: LlamaParse still reported the jobs SUCCESS but had
garbage-collected the markdown long before anyone asked for it.

13 of the 27 are EDUC 475.

### Why it is worse than it looks

The tutor answers these topics **confidently and in detail, and the detail is
fabricated.** A VIPKID question on 08-12 returned 12 citations and specifics —
700,000+ students, ~80% market share, the 2021 Double Reduction policy, a May
2015 refund policy with a 2% refund rate. None of that is in the corpus.
Retrieval matched on filenames; Sonnet supplied the content from its own
knowledge and cited your course documents for it.

Same for the PESTEL answer, and for "you're currently in Week 6, the
Brazil/Geekie week" — Week 6's entire stored body is the string
`"Week 6 The Brazil Education Market: Week 6"`.

This is worse than a visibly broken demo. It passes casual inspection and fails
the moment someone who knows the case asks a follow-up.

### It is also the root cause of the empty learner_signals

No body text → no concepts extracted → no COVERS edges → both signal-target
fallbacks return `[]` → capture skips the turn. Every other fix on that path
(§3) was necessary but not sufficient because of this.

### What to do

1. **Re-parse.** Needs `CRON_SECRET`, re-bills LlamaParse for 27 documents.
   Retry markers are already clear, so `scripts/fix-lecture-extraction.sh`
   picks them up with no manual reset. The poller is alive now
   (`llamaparse-poller-2m`, 59 successful runs in the two hours before writing),
   so it will not fall into the same hole.
2. **Widen the list first if needed.** The stub census above is EDUC 475 only.
   Run this to see whether other demo courses are affected:

   ```sql
   select
     coalesce(course_id, '(no course)') as course,
     count(*) as docs,
     count(*) filter (where length(coalesce(normalized_text,'')) < 400) as stubs,
     round(100.0 * count(*) filter (where length(coalesce(normalized_text,'')) < 400) / count(*)) as pct_stub
   from normalized_events
   where cancelled_at is null
     and source_type in ('canvas_lecture','canvas_file','canvas_syllabus','canvas_assignment','canvas_home')
   group by 1 order by stubs desc;
   ```

3. **Then verify** the text landed, and re-test whether signals capture.

### The fix that stops it recurring

All 29 stubs are marked `pipeline_version_v4: true`. The pipeline ran happily
over filename-only bodies and marked them done. **A minimum-body-length guard at
ingest** — refuse to mark extracted below a threshold, leave the retry marker
set — turns three silent weeks into a visible failure the same day. Not built.
